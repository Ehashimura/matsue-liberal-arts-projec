What this dashboard is for

この記録の目的

The dashboard brings together the small records generated through weekly teaching: what was taught, what was examined more closely, what evidence was available, what was learned, and what will be tried next. It is not intended to score Robert's teaching. Its purpose is to make development visible and allow useful learning to accumulate across the project.
